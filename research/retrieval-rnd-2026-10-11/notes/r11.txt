R11 — Adaptive routing, search policy learning, and budgets
Verified 2026-10-11. Recommendation: use a deterministic cheap-first planner initially, with semantic retrieval selected independently of lexical score; learn a routing policy only after collecting task-level outcomes. This is a proposed Lumen design, not a demonstrated benchmark result.

Primary evidence
1. Jeong et al., Adaptive-RAG, NAACL, June 2024. https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.389/ (PDF checked: https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.389.pdf). A trained T5-Large classifier selects no retrieval, single-step retrieval, or iterative retrieval according to query complexity. Labels combine model outcomes and dataset inductive biases. Training queries are disjoint from QA testing. This supports allocation by task complexity, not a single relevance-score threshold. Gains trade quality against time: results do not establish that adaptive retrieval beats single retrieval in latency on every query. Evaluation uses factual QA, older generators, A100 hardware, and single runs. A pretrained general model plus handwritten router does not reproduce this trained classifier. For private repository facts, its “no retrieval” action should translate to exact indexed lookup or cache reuse with freshness checks, not answering from model memory.

2. Jin et al., Search-R1, COLM 2025; initial preprint March 12, 2025. https://arxiv.org/html/2503.09516v5 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09516 . RL trains the generator to interleave reasoning and multiple search calls; retrieval-result tokens are masked out of the training objective. The final paper reports 24% / 20% average relative improvement for Qwen2.5-7B / 3B against its compared baselines across seven QA datasets (not percentage-point gains and not Lumen/grep speedups). The default uses outcome exact-match reward and PPO. This motivates eventual policy learning from successful repository tasks, but requires training compute, rewardable examples, and a compatible search environment. More autonomous searches can increase latency, token cost, and false paths. Ranking improvement and search-policy improvement must be evaluated separately. Prefer the final paper's numbers: early preprint abstracts report different percentages.

3. McCleary & Ghawaly, Quantifying the Accuracy and Cost Impact of Design Decisions in Budget-Constrained Agentic LLM Search, LREC, May 2026. https://aclanthology.org/2026.lrec-1.808/ (PDF checked). BCAS exposes remaining searches and completion tokens and enforces caps without fine-tuning. Across six LLMs and three QA benchmarks, extra search helps up to a small cap; hybrid lexical+dense retrieval plus reranking is the strongest average component in its ablation. The reranking condition considers 100 candidates but emits five chunks. This supports candidate breadth with narrow final context. Limits: component ablations center on HotpotQA, factual static corpora differ from repositories, direct non-agentic comparisons are future work, and runtime accounting excludes queueing/batching. Do not advertise a universal optimal search count or speedup from this paper.

Local implementation hypothesis
Route exact symbols/paths/literals to lexical or structural indexes. Route natural-language intent and paraphrases to both lexical and semantic candidate generation immediately, including when BM25 returns a confident but wrong lexical cluster. If embeddings are unavailable, visibly use lexical query expansion; do not claim equivalent semantic recall. Deduplicate and rerank the union; select compact evidence with source spans. Escalate unresolved relational questions to bounded reference/call/dependency expansion and one targeted reformulation. Carry a remaining budget and an explicit list of unresolved evidence needs. Stop when required evidence is covered or measured marginal relevant novelty stalls; return uncertainty when a hard budget expires. Treat this zero-training policy and all thresholds as hypotheses. Confidence should reflect held-out calibration, evidence coverage, disagreement and freshness, not raw BM25 magnitude or an LLM's uncalibrated certainty.

Strict ablation
Fixed held-out tasks stratified by exact lookup, paraphrase, misleading vocabulary, multi-hop and stale files; hold generator, corpus snapshot and emitted context-token ceiling constant. Compare lexical-only; always-hybrid; naive BM25-threshold fallback; independent query-type routing; routing plus graph expansion; routing plus iterative reformulation. Sweep equal wall-time and total-token budgets, including planner/reranker/embedding calls and index amortization. Measure evidence recall, answer/task success, duplicate/irrelevant context tokens, p50/p95 latency and dollars. Report difficult-query regressions, routing mistakes and confidence calibration separately. A learned router must be trained on a separate split and compared to both heuristic and oracle routing. Ship only if the Pareto frontier improves without hiding failed hard queries behind early stopping.
