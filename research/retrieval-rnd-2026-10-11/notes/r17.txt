R17/25 — Decision-theoretic retrieval control (verified 2026-10-11)

Recommendation: adopt a capped retrieval controller now; treat learned value-of-information routing as a research extension. This improves the number/order of searches and reads, rather than inventing another index.

Verified primary literature
1. Hay, Russell, Tolpin & Shimony, “Selecting Computations: Theory and Applications,” UAI, 14–18 August 2012. https://proceedings.mlr.press/r10/hay12a.html ; full paper https://arxiv.org/pdf/1207.5879 . Models computation as evidence acquisition with a cost, and stopping as selecting the highest-utility terminal action. Their experiments concern selection and Go, not document retrieval. Their key warning transfers directly: ordinary cumulative-reward bandits do not model within-task deliberation correctly; a one-step value-of-information policy can stop too early when several computations are jointly useful. Thus graph expansion followed by reading should sometimes be evaluated as a bundled action.
2. Li, Chu, Langford & Schapire, “A Contextual-Bandit Approach to Personalized News Article Recommendation,” WWW, 26 April 2010. https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.0146 . Evaluated on over 33 million Yahoo! events; 12.5% click lift over a context-free bandit. This supports efficient contextual routing across repeated tasks, not a claimed retrieval-quality gain. Contextual bandits fit choosing a complete retrieval recipe for each new query better than assigning immediate reward to every search inside one dependent trajectory.
3. Dudík, Langford & Li, “Doubly Robust Policy Evaluation and Learning,” ICML, June 2011. https://icml.cc/2011/papers/554_icmlpaper.pdf . Combines reward prediction and inverse propensity correction, analyzes bias/variance, and evaluates classification/portal data. Logged actions are selectively observed; replaying old deterministic logs cannot reveal outcomes of never-chosen actions. Doubly robust estimation still requires action support and suitable assumptions, and does not remove severe small-propensity variance.

Concrete adaptation (proposal, unvalidated)
Let s contain query features, retrieved/read evidence IDs, symbol/path matches, source freshness, lexical/semantic disagreement, unresolved claims, and remaining budgets. Actions are lexical expansion, semantic retrieval, graph expansion, read, or stop. Let U(s) be expected terminal task correctness with valid evidence, not score entropy or result count. Choose action maximizing E[V(s')|s,a] − lambda_time·latency(a) − lambda_tokens·tokens(a), versus U(s) for stop; V includes subsequent actions. Costs should include controller overhead. Unknown evidence utility is learned from adjudicated outcomes, with uncertainty retained; a low-entropy ranking is not evidence sufficiency.

Near term: deterministic routing with explicit budgets. Exact identifiers start lexical; weak/no matches or vocabulary mismatch trigger one semantic pass; unresolved symbol relationships trigger bounded graph expansion plus a read. Read top deduplicated spans under a token cap. Stop on budget exhaustion or evidence-completeness criteria validated on held-out tasks. Return unresolved claims when the cap is reached. Do not call arbitrary score thresholds calibrated probabilities.

Later: collect randomized recipe-selection logs on an offline benchmark (or explicitly approved traffic), recording context, chosen recipe, exact propensity, versioned corpus, costs and adjudicated final outcome. Learn a lightweight contextual router; use held-out or cross-fitted reward models and doubly robust evaluation with overlap/variance diagnostics. A sequential policy needs trajectory-aware evaluation; one-step bandit correction does not validate it.

Falsifiable test: compare fixed lexical, always-hybrid, capped heuristic, and learned recipe routing with identical retrievers/readers. Include real paraphrases, ambiguous names, empty-answer queries, naturally irrelevant documents, and cross-file questions; synthetic corruption alone is insufficient. Split by repository and time. Hypothesis: at matched evidence-supported task accuracy, routing reduces median latency or context tokens ≥20%, without worse p95 latency or increased unsupported answers. Report bootstrap intervals, false-stop rate, calibration by query type, controller cost, and ablations; reject if savings vanish after overhead or accuracy declines materially.
