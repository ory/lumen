1. Primary-source checks
SPLADE-Code supports learned sparse expansion as a serious candidate, not a universal improvement. Appendix C explicitly excludes query encoding; its second-stage latency is estimated from sparse dot products. Section 4.3 compares Seismic and FAISS on one CPU and a particular CodeSearchNet workload. Section 6 acknowledges missing realistic developer-query and end-to-end agent evaluation. Therefore “sub-millisecond search” must always mean index lookup here, never complete local semantic search. The proposed FTS expansion baseline is correctly distinguished from learned sparse dot-product scoring. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2603.22008v1#A3

Adaptive-RAG establishes routing among no-retrieval, single-step and iterative retrieval for open-domain QA, using a learned query-complexity classifier. It does not establish the proposed lexical/vector/graph scheduler, tool-token savings, code correctness, or its training-data requirements. Retain “motivates” and explicitly label transfer to code-agent work an inference. Source: https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.389.pdf

Neither source establishes novelty for this combination. “Research contribution” should mean proposed future investigation, not a result of this report. Twenty-five AI assignments provide coverage, not independent empirical replications or institutional endorsement.

4. Query-plan adoption gate
The three-repeat SQL probe and matching API outputs support a severe plan bottleneck on this 12,292-chunk corpus. They do not establish FTS-first as universally optimal. Before shipping, compare the original planner after ANALYZE/appropriate statistics refresh against explicit order. Cross rare/common terms with absent/broad/selective path filters and small/large active worktrees inside a shared collection. Include many nonmember/stale revisions, duplicate content mappings, changed/deleted files and zero-result cases.

Require filters before LIMIT, identical score boosts and deterministic tie order, no duplicate mappings, correct current-worktree hashes, and equivalent complete metadata. Measure supported SQLite builds, cold/warm p50/p95 and concurrent update behavior; avoid an unconditional forced plan if filter-first wins selective cases. No ANN/WAND replacement is justified while this execution-plan defect dominates.
